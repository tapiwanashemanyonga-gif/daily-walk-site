## Product truth — keep every post accurate

Daily Walk is being developed as a Christian spiritual-growth companion. The planned experience includes Bible reading and study support, an AI Bible guide, short devotionals, Scripture memory practice, sermon/study preparation, prayer resources and Bible listening.

Do not claim that a feature, translation, offline library, or service is available unless it has been verified in the current public app build. In particular:
- Shona and Ndebele Bible translations are desired additions; do not advertise them as included until they are actually integrated and checked.
- The current signed APK on GitHub is labelled a **pre-release validation build**. Do not promote it as the public download or direct general users to install it.
- Do not promise pastoral, medical, legal or emergency counselling from AI responses. Encourage users to check teaching against Scripture and seek trusted human guidance where appropriate.
- Do not claim Google Play availability unless the app is actually published there.

## Core message

Make time for God's Word, prayer and reflection. Daily Walk is being built to make everyday spiritual growth easier to approach, understand and practise.

## 3 content pillars

1. **Daily time with Scripture:** short reflections, understanding passages and putting biblical teaching into practice.
2. **Prayer and spiritual growth:** building a consistent prayer habit, reflection and Scripture memory.
3. **Learning and serving:** preparing Bible studies, sermons and lessons with care and checking everything against Scripture.

## 20-second vertical video script

**0–3 sec — Hook:** “Finding it hard to stay consistent with your daily Bible time?”  
**3–7 sec — Show:** Show the Daily Walk website and app screens only where the feature is present in the current build.  
**7–12 sec — Explain:** “Daily Walk is being built to bring Scripture, devotionals, prayer and Bible study support into one simple experience.”  
**12–16 sec — Show:** Demonstrate one real, verified feature. Keep personal information and notifications hidden.  
**16–20 sec — CTA:** “Take one step in your faith journey today. Visit the Daily Walk website.”

Use original footage and only show features that are working in the build being promoted. Add readable captions and keep the phone screen visible.
