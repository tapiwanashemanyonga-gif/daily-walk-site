Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Daily Walk public website (the “Website”) handles information. It applies to this information website only. Features within the Daily Walk Android application may involve additional data practices, which should be explained in an app-specific privacy notice.

Information you provide

The Website does not currently provide account registration, a contact form or a payment form. If you email us at dailywalk.app1@gmail.com, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message, so that we can respond.

Technical information

The Website is hosted by Render. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, request time and server logs to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the Website. Please read Render’s Privacy Policy for details about its practices.

Anonymous website analytics

The Website may record page views and selected early-access or download-link clicks using a temporary random session identifier stored in your browser session storage. It does not intentionally collect your name, email address, IP address, prayer entries, Bible searches or reading history through this analytics feature. Events are sent to a Google Apps Script endpoint and stored in a private Google Sheet controlled by Daily Walk. Google may process technical request information when providing this service; see Google’s Privacy Policy. Website session identifiers are temporary and are not used to identify you across separate browser sessions.

Cookies and advertising

The public Website does not intentionally set advertising cookies or run third-party advertising scripts. Third-party websites linked from this Website have their own privacy policies.

How information is used

Information sent to us by email is used to respond to your request and manage project communications. We do not sell the contents of emails sent to us.

Children’s privacy

The Website provides general informational content and does not knowingly request personal information from children through forms.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Website changes. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact dailywalk.app1@gmail.com.
