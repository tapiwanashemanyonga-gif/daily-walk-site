A simple daily rhythm

Small moments. A stronger walk.

You do not have to know everything or do everything at once. Begin with one passage, one prayer and one step of faith today.

01

Read with an open heart

Spend time in Scripture and ask what it teaches about God and life.

02

Understand and reflect

Use helpful study resources to think through the message and its meaning.

03

Pray and put it into practice

Talk to God and choose one practical way to live out what you have learned.
